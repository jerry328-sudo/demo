      About the model

      Column height follows local thermal power. The physics is point kinetics with one delayed-neutron group (β = 0.5%, prompt generation time 0.8 ms) driven by per-channel steam void, fuel temperature (Doppler) and iodine-135 → xenon-135 poisoning. Steam void adds reactivity, as it did in the RBMK, and the effect is strongest at low power with coolant near boiling.

      Coefficients are tuned so the 1986 sequence plays out qualitatively as described in IAEA INSAG-7. Numbers are illustrative, not a reconstruction of the plant.
